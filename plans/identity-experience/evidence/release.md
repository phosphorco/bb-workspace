## Authority and service isolation assessment

The [master plan](../../../fork/plans/bb-fork-master-plan.md:120) selects trusted collaborators and trusted backend plugins, with equal information access; tenant isolation and a plugin sandbox are outside scope. Stronger process/OS least-authority isolation is a separately scoped architecture decision, not a silent prerequisite for identity UX completion.

Explicit grants must still identify principal/issuer, operation/resource scope, audience, expiry and revocation behavior when delegated authority is proposed. Current request-bound capability is not a durable general delegation grant: ordinary background work may carry initiating-person provenance separately from its executing actor and needs no new delegation grant. Issuer trust belongs to configured boundary providers plus validated ingress, never names, client principal IDs or fabricated headers. Configured identity failure must degrade to carried or machine attribution for ordinary work; it must not fabricate a verified person. [Master §§5.1–5.2](../../../fork/plans/bb-fork-master-plan.md:217).

The preview's [native identity contract](/home/ubuntu/bb-service/fork/plans/native-identity-settings-and-authorship.md:33) says live validity is checked before acceptance, while accepted queue snapshots may drain after the request ends. Revocation/retired generation invalidates prior person evidence; new ordinary work resolves carried or machine attribution; it does not rewrite accepted authorship or automatically cancel durable jobs. Cancellation of accepted work needs an explicit supported operation and policy. Reconciliation reads cannot authorize a fresh send; unknown receipt lookup cannot justify a new operation ID.

Execution filesystem powers are separate from identity: shared Unix-user processes may read operator files. The [proof isolation record](../../../fork/plans/bb-fork-local-proof.md:133) explicitly says clean environment/empty state is operational isolation and its attempted filesystem restriction did not establish a sandbox. Do not describe separate ports, service units, or request attribution as filesystem confinement. Any new provider process boundary must prove credential-limited real transport, invalidation/expiry during asynchronous work, commit-boundary validation, crash/retry/duplicate handling and unchanged state after rejection. The [package transaction contract](../../../plugins/packages/bb-identity/README.md:316) is synchronous same-process; it explicitly requires a separately proven protocol for remote storage/host processes.
